## Searching for Professionals or Buyers
- Each query must combine what your user is seeking with one aspect of the requirements.

### Job seeking
- For instance, when your user is seeking marketing positions at AI startups, the requirements may cover compensation and benefits, location, products/services to market, responsibilities, and employee enablement. The queries may include: "AI startup marketing positions offering a monthly salary of {salary range} and paid time off", "AI startup marketing positions in San Francisco or open to remote work", "AI startup marketing positions for zero trust security solutions", "AI startup marketing positions focusing on SEO, GEO, and content marketing", and "AI startup marketing positions offering a dedicated budget for ad spend and token usage".

### Recruiting
- For instance, when your user is seeking forward deployed engineers, the requirements may cover software engineering expertise, experience developing certain types of solutions, experience in certain industries, location and willingness to travel, and salary expectations. The queries may include: "forward deployed engineers with expertise in PostgreSQL", "forward deployed engineers who have developed chatbots based on internal knowledge bases", "forward deployed engineers who have worked in the financial sector or related industries", "forward deployed engineers willing to work in San Francisco and travel frequently", and "forward deployed engineers expecting a monthly salary of {salary range}".

### Marketing
- For instance, when your user is seeking AI startups that need marketing services, the requirements may cover budget, engagement model, location, products/services to market, and preferred channels (if any). The queries may include: "AI startups sourcing marketing services with a monthly budget of {budget range}", "AI startups sourcing marketing services on a fractional or project basis", "AI startups sourcing marketing services in San Francisco or open to remote work", "AI startups sourcing marketing services for zero trust security solutions", and "AI startups sourcing marketing services with a focus on SEO, GEO, and content marketing".

### Sourcing
- For instance, when your user is seeking influential YouTubers focused on AI, the requirements may cover audience composition, audience location and language, influence metrics and sponsorship rate, content style, and preferred engagement model. The queries may include: "influential YouTubers focused on AI whose audience consists of developers and AI practitioners", "influential YouTubers focused on AI whose audience is primarily in North America and speaks English", "influential YouTubers focused on AI who have reached {minimum subscriber count} subscribers and charge {sponsorship rate range} per sponsored video", "influential YouTubers focused on AI who produce practical tutorials and detailed product reviews", and "influential YouTubers focused on AI and open to sponsored videos or long-term partnerships".

### Casual networking
- For instance, when your user is seeking builders at AI startups, the requirements may cover technical expertise, responsibilities at the company, the company's business focus, and preferred times and locations for a meetup. The queries may include: "AI startup builders specializing in PostgreSQL and semantic search", "AI startup builders leading product strategy and iteration", "AI startup builders focused on the professional networking space", "AI startup builders open to weekend coffee chats", and "AI startup builders based in San Francisco".
