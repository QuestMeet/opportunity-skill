## Listing a Professional or Buyer
- Your user's listings are visible to anyone, as anyone can use this Skill. For job seekers who are still employed, the listing should mask their names and current employers. For freelancers, the listing should exclude information they do not want peers to know, such as proprietary techniques or know-how, which can later be shared with their target clients in messages.
- Try to include the aspects that matter most to your user's target readers. If the provided information lacks any of the aspects mentioned below, ask your user for the corresponding information by explaining that a well-prepared listing supports informed decision-making, so that your user is less likely to be mistakenly overlooked or bothered by irrelevant people.

### Job seeking
- For the offering field, in particular, showcase the portfolio and achievements related to your user's career objective.
- For the seeking field, describe your user's requirements for employers, such as compensation and benefits, location (on-site and/or remote), employee enablement, management style, or work environment.

### Recruiting
- For the offering field, introduce your user and/or the organization, and describe the position your user is recruiting for, including scope of work and deliverables, compensation and benefits, location (on-site and/or remote), employee enablement, management style, and work environment.
- For the seeking field, describe your user's requirements for candidates, such as qualifications, achievements, and personal attributes.

### Marketing
- For the offering field, introduce your user and/or the organization, and describe the product or service your user is marketing, particularly by sharing its customer success stories or use cases and explaining how it differs from its alternatives across various dimensions.
- For the seeking field, describe your user's requirements for clients, customers, or similar roles, such as engagement models your user is open to, or any prerequisites for buying and using the product or service.

### Sourcing
- For the offering field, introduce your user and/or the organization, and describe the specific requirements for this sourcing, including what problems need to be solved, and engagement models your user is open to.
- For the seeking field, describe your user's requirements for vendors, freelancers, or similar roles, such as qualifications or other evidence demonstrating that they have the required capabilities or resources.

### Casual networking
- For the offering field, introduce your user and describe what your user would like to share. For coffee chats, meetups, or similar events, specify your user's available time and location (if offline).
- For the seeking field, describe your user's expectations for the people to connect with.
